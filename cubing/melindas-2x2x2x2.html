Solving Melinda’s 2×2×2×2

My solution to Melinda Green’s 2×2×2×2 is listed as entry #68 in her Hall of Fame, dated May 14, 2024. The number marks my place in the accepted-solution list, not a speed ranking.

A four-dimensional puzzle, in your hands

Melinda’s magnetic puzzle physically realizes the four-dimensional counterpart of a 2×2×2 cube. Its “gyro” move exchanges the outer axis with one of the other three, making the full 4D state space accessible through physical moves. Her project page explains the design and legal moves.
